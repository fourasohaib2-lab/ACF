\section{ACF Space Weather Architecture}


The ACF Space Weather module extends the Earth system digital
twin beyond the atmosphere by including the heliosphere and
near-Earth space environment.



The architecture includes:


\begin{itemize}

\item Solar observation layer

\item Solar wind monitoring

\item Magnetosphere modelling

\item Ionosphere analysis

\item Space weather forecasting

\item Impact assessment

\end{itemize}



The complete chain is:


\begin{equation}
Sun
\rightarrow
Solar Wind
\rightarrow
Magnetosphere
\rightarrow
Ionosphere
\rightarrow
Earth Systems
\end{equation}
