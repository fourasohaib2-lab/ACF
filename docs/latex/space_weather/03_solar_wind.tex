\section{Solar Wind Dynamics}


The solar wind is a continuous plasma flow emitted by the Sun.



Main parameters:


\begin{itemize}

\item Velocity

\item Density

\item Temperature

\item Magnetic field

\item Plasma composition

\end{itemize}



The solar wind pressure is:


\begin{equation}
P_{dyn}
=
\rho V^2
\end{equation}
