\section{ACF Operational Center Architecture}


The ACF Operational Meteorological Center provides a complete
environment for monitoring, forecasting and decision support.



The center includes:


\begin{itemize}

\item Data acquisition systems

\item Numerical prediction systems

\item AI forecasting engines

\item Visualization platforms

\item Warning management systems

\item Communication infrastructure

\end{itemize}



Operational chain:


\begin{equation}
Observation
\rightarrow
Analysis
\rightarrow
Forecast
\rightarrow
Warning
\rightarrow
Decision
\end{equation}
