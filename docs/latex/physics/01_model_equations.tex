\section{Primitive Atmospheric Equations}


The ACF atmospheric solver is based on the fundamental conservation
equations of geophysical fluid dynamics.



\subsection{Continuity Equation}


\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}
+
\nabla \cdot (\rho \vec{V})
=
0
\end{equation}



\subsection{Momentum Equation}


\begin{equation}
\frac{D\vec{V}}{Dt}
=
-\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p
-2\vec{\Omega}\times\vec{V}
+\vec{g}
+\vec{F}
\end{equation}


where $\vec{\Omega}$ is Earth's rotation vector and $\vec{F}$ represents
frictional (viscous/turbulent) forces (Wallace \& Hobbs, Eq. 7.9).



\subsection{Thermodynamic Equation}

The first law of thermodynamics for a moist parcel, in flux form, retains
the adiabatic compression/expansion term omitted by a bare $DT/Dt=Q/c_p$:

\begin{equation}
\frac{DT}{Dt}
=
\frac{Q}{c_p}
+
\frac{RT}{c_p p}\frac{Dp}{Dt}
\end{equation}


Equivalently, in terms of potential temperature $\theta$ (which absorbs the
adiabatic term exactly, Wallace \& Hobbs, Eq. 3.53):

\begin{equation}
\frac{D\theta}{Dt}
=
\frac{\theta}{T}\frac{Q}{c_p}
\end{equation}



\subsection{Water Vapor Equation}


\begin{equation}
\frac{Dq_v}{Dt}
=
S_q
\end{equation}



The complete atmospheric state vector is:


\begin{equation}
X=
(u,v,w,T,p,q_v,q_c,q_r)
\end{equation}
