\section{Primitive Atmospheric Equations}


The ACF atmospheric solver is based on the fundamental conservation
equations of geophysical fluid dynamics.



\subsection{Continuity Equation}


\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}
+
\nabla \cdot (\rho \vec{V})
=
0
\end{equation}



\subsection{Momentum Equation}


\begin{equation}
\frac{D\vec{V}}{Dt}
=
-\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p
-2\Omega\times V
+g
+F
\end{equation}



\subsection{Thermodynamic Equation}


\begin{equation}
\frac{DT}{Dt}
=
\frac{Q}{C_p}
\end{equation}



\subsection{Water Vapor Equation}


\begin{equation}
\frac{Dq_v}{Dt}
=
S_q
\end{equation}



The complete atmospheric state vector is:


\begin{equation}
X=
(u,v,w,T,p,q_v,q_c,q_r)
\end{equation}
