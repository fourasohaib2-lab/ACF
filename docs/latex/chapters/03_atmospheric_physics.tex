\section{Fundamental Atmospheric Equations}

The atmosphere is governed by the conservation laws of mass,
momentum and energy.


\subsection{Mass Conservation}

The continuity equation describes the conservation of atmospheric mass:

\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}
+
\nabla \cdot (\rho \vec{V})
=
0
\end{equation}

where:

\begin{itemize}
\item $\rho$ is the air density
\item $\vec{V}$ is the three-dimensional wind vector
\item $t$ is time
\end{itemize}



\subsection{Momentum Conservation}

The atmospheric momentum equation is:

\begin{equation}
\frac{D\vec{V}}{Dt}
=
-\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p
+
\vec{g}
-
2\vec{\Omega}\times\vec{V}
+
\vec{F}
\end{equation}


where:

\begin{itemize}
\item $p$ is atmospheric pressure
\item $\vec{g}$ is gravity
\item $\Omega$ represents Earth's rotation
\item $\vec{F}$ represents frictional forces
\end{itemize}



\subsection{Thermodynamic Energy Equation}

The temperature evolution follows the first law of thermodynamics; the
adiabatic compression/expansion term $\frac{RT}{c_p p}\frac{Dp}{Dt}$ must be
retained alongside the diabatic term (it vanishes only when the equation is
written for potential temperature $\theta$ instead of $T$, Wallace \& Hobbs
Eq. 3.53):

\begin{equation}
\frac{DT}{Dt}
=
\frac{Q}{c_p}
+
\frac{RT}{c_p p}\frac{Dp}{Dt}
\end{equation}

where:

\begin{itemize}
\item $T$ is temperature
\item $Q$ represents diabatic heating
\item $c_p$ is the specific heat capacity
\item $R$ is the specific gas constant for (moist) air and $Dp/Dt$ is the
  parcel's pressure tendency
\end{itemize}



\subsection{Equation of State}

The atmosphere follows the ideal gas law:

\begin{equation}
p=\rho R_d T
\end{equation}

where $R_d$ is the specific gas constant for dry air.



\section{Hydrostatic Balance}

For large-scale atmospheric motion:

\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial p}{\partial z}
=
-\rho g
\end{equation}



\section{Geophysical Fluid Dynamics}

Atmospheric circulation is influenced by:

\begin{itemize}
\item Coriolis force
\item Pressure gradients
\item Turbulence
\item Convection
\item Radiation
\end{itemize}
