The Atmospheric Complexity Framework (ACF) is a next generation
Earth system intelligence platform.

Atmospheric dynamics are governed by complex physical processes
described in classical meteorological theory
\cite{holton2004,wallace2006}.
The objective of ACF is to integrate:

\begin{itemize}
\item Atmospheric observation
\item Numerical weather prediction
\item Artificial intelligence
\item Digital twin technologies
\item Operational decision support
\end{itemize}
