\section{Sea Surface Temperature}


Sea Surface Temperature (SST) is a fundamental variable of the
Earth climate system.



ACF monitors:


\begin{itemize}

\item Global SST fields

\item Ocean heat anomalies

\item Marine heat waves

\item El Niño and La Niña signals

\end{itemize}
