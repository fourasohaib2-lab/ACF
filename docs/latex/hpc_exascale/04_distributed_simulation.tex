\section{Distributed Simulation Engine}


The ACF simulation engine distributes complex Earth system
calculations across multiple computing resources.



Distributed components:


\begin{itemize}

\item Atmospheric models

\item Ocean models

\item Climate models

\item Digital Twin simulations

\item AI models

\end{itemize}



Distributed workflow:


\[
Domain\ Decomposition
\rightarrow
Parallel\ Computation
\rightarrow
Model\ Coupling
\]
