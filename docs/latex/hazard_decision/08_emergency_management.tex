\section{Emergency Management System}

\subsection{Executive Summary}

The ACF Emergency Management System is an operational decision support
framework designed to assist authorities before, during and after
natural hazard events.

The system connects scientific prediction with emergency operations.

The objectives are:

\begin{itemize}

\item Early warning

\item Crisis monitoring

\item Emergency coordination

\item Decision assistance

\item Disaster recovery support

\end{itemize}


The ACF Emergency System integrates:

\begin{itemize}

\item Hazard prediction modules

\item AWCI operational center

\item Risk mapping engine

\item Artificial intelligence

\item Earth System Digital Twin

\item Communication services

\end{itemize}
