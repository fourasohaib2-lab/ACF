\section{Specific Humidity}


Specific humidity represents the mass of water vapor per unit
mass of moist air.


\[
q_v=\frac{m_v}{m}
\]


Unit:

\[
kg/kg
\]



\section{Relative Humidity}


Relative humidity is:


\begin{equation}
RH=
\frac{e}{e_s}
\times100
\end{equation}


Applications:

\begin{itemize}

\item Cloud formation

\item Precipitation prediction

\item Atmospheric moisture transport

\end{itemize}



\section{Saturation Vapor Pressure}


The saturation vapor pressure $e_s$ (hPa) as a function of temperature
$T$ (\si{\celsius}) follows the Bolton (1980) fit to the
Clausius-Clapeyron relation:

\begin{equation}
e_s(T) = 6.112 \exp\left(\frac{17.67\,T}{T+243.5}\right)
\end{equation}



\section{Virtual Temperature}


Virtual temperature $T_v$ is the temperature dry air would need to have
the same density as moist air at the same pressure:

\begin{equation}
T_v = T\,(1 + 0.61\,q_v)
\end{equation}


where $q_v$ is specific humidity (kg/kg).
