\section{Specific Humidity}


Specific humidity represents the mass of water vapor per unit
mass of moist air.


\[
q_v=\frac{m_v}{m}
\]


Unit:

\[
kg/kg
\]



\section{Relative Humidity}


Relative humidity is:


\begin{equation}
RH=
\frac{e}{e_s}
\times100
\end{equation}


Applications:

\begin{itemize}

\item Cloud formation

\item Precipitation prediction

\item Atmospheric moisture transport

\end{itemize}
