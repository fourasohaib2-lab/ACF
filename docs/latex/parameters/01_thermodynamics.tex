\section{Temperature}

Temperature represents the thermal state of the atmosphere.

Symbol:

\[
T
\]

Unit:

\[
K
\]

Applications:

\begin{itemize}
\item Weather forecasting
\item Stability analysis
\item Climate studies
\end{itemize}



\section{Pressure}

Atmospheric pressure is the force exerted by the atmospheric column.

Symbol:

\[
p
\]

Unit:

\[
Pa
\]


Hydrostatic equation:

\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial p}{\partial z}
=
-\rho g
\end{equation}



\section{Potential Temperature}

Potential temperature is defined as:

\begin{equation}
\theta =
T
\left(
\frac{p_0}{p}
\right)^{\frac{R_d}{c_p}}
\end{equation}


Applications:

\begin{itemize}

\item Atmospheric stability

\item Air mass identification

\item Convection analysis

\end{itemize}
