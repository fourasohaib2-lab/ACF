\section{ACF Atmospheric Chemistry Architecture}


The ACF atmospheric chemistry module represents the chemical
state of the atmosphere and its interactions with weather,
climate and human activities.



The architecture integrates:


\begin{itemize}

\item Chemical observations

\item Emission inventories

\item Transport models

\item Atmospheric reactions

\item Air quality prediction

\item Climate interactions

\end{itemize}



Chemical system workflow:


\begin{equation}
Emission
\rightarrow
Transport
\rightarrow
Reaction
\rightarrow
Concentration
\rightarrow
Impact
\end{equation}
