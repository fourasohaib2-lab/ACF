\section{Chemical Transport Models}


Chemical transport models simulate the evolution of atmospheric
species.



The model solves:


\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial C}{\partial t}
+
\nabla(V C)
=
S
+
R
\end{equation}



where:


\begin{itemize}

\item $C$ : concentration

\item $V$ : atmospheric transport

\item $S$ : emissions

\item $R$ : chemical reactions

\end{itemize}
