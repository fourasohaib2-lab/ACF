# AWCI SP7 sounding fixtures

- `igra2-station-list-excerpt.txt`: five unmodified lines of the IGRA v2 station list
  (NOAA NCEI, https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/integrated-global-radiosonde-archive/doc/igra2-station-list.txt,
  downloaded 2026-09-26; U.S. Government work, public domain). Chosen to cover an active WMO station inside the test
  domain (Dar-El-Beida 60390), an inactive one (Annaba 60360), a non-WMO id (AGXUAE04843), an active station
  elsewhere in North Africa (Casablanca 60155) and one outside the domain (Broome 94203).
- `uwyo_60390_2026092500.csv`: University of Wyoming sounding of Dar-El-Beida (Algiers), nominal 2026-09-25 00 UTC
  (launch 2026-09-24 23:31 UTC), as returned by weather.uwyo.edu/wsgi/sounding?…&type=TEXT:CSV, unmodified.
  Courtesy of the University of Wyoming, Department of Atmospheric Science.
