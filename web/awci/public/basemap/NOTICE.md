# Basemap

Natural Earth 1:50m vector data (public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/),
layers ne_50m_land, ne_50m_coastline, ne_50m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land, from github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector,
clipped to lon -30.0..50.0, lat 5.0..55.0 and rounded to 3 decimals on 2026-09-25 by tools/awci/make_basemap.py.
